LUCIFER LAUNCHER V2 · UI DIRECTION

Liquid Crystal — 10 brand-new tab screens

A complete visual rebuild of every tab page in your CS Launcher V2 fork. The old left icon rail is gone, the white pill LAUNCH button is gone, the flat widget cards are gone. Everything now floats as thick frosted glass over a deep-space aurora, with liquid light cores, chromatic edges and a bottom orb dock. No feature was removed — every item that exists today has an exact new home (see the parity table at the end).

Design system — Liquid Crystal

how to build it

Surfaces

  • Base: void black #06070F + aurora nebula, bokeh embers, caustic haze, blurred voxel horizon
  • Panel: white at 5–9% over the backdrop, 24 dp squircle radius, 1 px white rim at 14–20%
  • Blur: 18–28 dp backdrop blur, saturation ~140%, plus a top-left specular highlight
  • Accent rim: 1 px cyan→magenta gradient, only on the focused/active element

Motion & light

  • Liquid cores: 2–3 layered translucent orbs with slow internal drift; they mark the current tab and the Launch action
  • Hover / press: 300 ms spring scale 1 → 1.04, rim brightens, caustic bloom grows
  • Transitions: sheets rise + fade with blur-in; the dock core slides between orbs
  • Progress always reads as light: orbiting halo on Launch, circular ring on downloads

Android implementation notes

  • Backdrop blur: RenderEffect.createBlurEffect (API 31+) or a RealtimeBlurView/Haze fallback below that; always pair with a translucent fallback colour so low-end devices still read correctly
  • Rim light: layer-list / radial gradient drawables instead of 9-patch, so rims scale with density
  • Dock: single FrameLayout + 5 animated orbs replaces the left rail in fragment_home_fastclient.xml
  • Launch orb: custom view with a rotating halo shader; hosts both launch and download progress states
  • Aurora backdrop: one static blurred asset + slow parallax layer beats a live shader for battery
